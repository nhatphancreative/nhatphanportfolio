Background

Life Insurance is often perceived as dry and complicated, built on numbers, clauses, and promises made by adults. In the process, the small dreams of children are often overlooked.

Insight

When the future is seen through a child's eyes, it becomes simple, pure, and full of possibility. Children don't think in terms of risk or financial security, they draw dreams, hopes, and the world they want to grow up in. And that innocence has a power no insurance terminology can match, it speaks directly to the hearts of adults.

Idea

The project uses drawings made by underprivileged children at a Hanwha Life CSR event, where they expressed their understanding of life insurance. These authentic illustrations become the campaign's key visuals and key messages across OOH and social media, turning children's perspectives into a powerful reminder of what life insurance truly means.

Social Activation

During International Children's Day week, Hanwha Life Vietnam will transform its brand identity with a playful coloring book inspired look, from avatars and cover photos to social visuals, launching the “Bao Hiem Cung De Thuong Ma” series to make insurance feel softer, warmer, and more approachable.

OOH

A series of playful OOH posters will also appear across the city, spreading the campaign's childlike art style and turning everyday spaces into little reminders that insurance can feel warm, friendly, and cute too.
