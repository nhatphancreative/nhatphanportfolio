Background & Insight

Cake by VPBank operates on one core philosophy: "Easy as cake". For Tet 2025 (Year of the Snake), the brand needed to break away from traditional, stiff corporate gifting. The concept, "CHẠM CAKE TÍ - SỐNG I-ZÌ" (With Cake, Life is EZ), reimagines the snake motif as smooth, flexible geometric shapes. It turns the typical financial stress of the Lunar New Year into a seamless, vibrant, and effortless experience for users.

Execution

Rather than conventional corporate gifting, we engineered a highly covetable "Tet Survival Kit" designed to seamlessly blend Cake into users' daily lives.

Social Activation

The collection was amplified through a series of Facebook social posts and community activations, including teasers, product showcases, and photoshoot content designed to spark conversation and encourage sharing during the Tet' season.
