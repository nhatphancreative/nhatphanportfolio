Background

Digibank is the bank of the future, for future generations, for Gen Z. However, digibank products, such as debit cards, are still similar to traditional banks, with boring design, which contradicts what it stands for Gen Z.

Insight

Gen Z craves personalization and self-expression, especially through music. Music brings rhythm and energy to every activity, connecting people together.

Idea

Turn your debit card into your favorite Spotify song of your own, no longer just a debit card, but a pocket-sized anthem that reflects your style, your vibe, and your connections. It's a part of who you are, sparking conversations and connections with everyone who gets a glimpse of it.

The deeper meaning

Real-life interactions are becoming increasingly rare, as most connections happen online. This debit card concept is designed to bring back that lost sense of human connection, while still allowing for individual expression. Gen Z craves personalization, and what better way to express themselves than through music?

This card doesn't just reflect personal style; it becomes a bridge to direct, meaningful interactions between people. Imagine your Cake debit card designed to look like your favorite Spotify song. Every time you use it, it catches the eye, sparking curiosity from those around you. Maybe the cashier notices the song and asks about it, turning a simple transaction into a conversation about music tastes and style. Or someone at a café notices the design and shares the same taste.

What was once a silent transaction now turns into a moment of connection - sparked by music.

OOH & Social Activation

A series of OOH posters will be deployed across the city, extending the campaign's visual identity and transforming everyday spaces into reminders to let your debit card DEBEAT!
